What I Work On

Research Projects

My lab investigates how intelligent systems can be made more transparent, fair, and beneficial — particularly in resource-constrained and high-stakes settings.

FairLearn: Bias Mitigation in Large Language Models

We study how stereotypes and demographic biases propagate through pre-trained language models and develop post-hoc and in-training debiasing techniques that preserve model utility while improving fairness across protected attributes.

LLMs Fairness Debiasing NLP

FedGraph: Federated Learning over Heterogeneous Graphs

This project develops privacy-preserving graph neural network training protocols for distributed healthcare and financial networks, enabling collaborative learning without sharing raw data across organizational boundaries.

Federated Learning GNNs Privacy Healthcare AI

ExplainX: Human-in-the-Loop Model Explanations

ExplainX bridges interpretable machine learning and user-centered design, producing explanations co-developed with domain experts to aid clinical decision support and automated auditing systems.

Interpretability HCI Clinical AI XAI

In the Classroom

Teaching

Teaching Philosophy

I believe great teaching happens at the boundary of theory and practice. My courses are designed to challenge students to think rigorously while building real-world skills. I emphasize collaborative problem-solving, peer review, and iterative project work.

"The goal of education is not to fill a bucket, but to light a fire — and in computer science, that fire should illuminate both the elegance of abstraction and the messiness of real data."

I am particularly committed to creating inclusive classrooms. I maintain flexible office hours, publish all course materials openly, and actively encourage students from underrepresented groups to pursue research opportunities in my lab.
